# Installing CHEMATRIX (for students)

You do **not** need an app store, an account, or any programming tools. You need the CHEMATRIX link (an `https://` address from your teacher) and an internet connection once.

## Android phone or tablet

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** if a banner appears. If not, tap the **⋮** menu (top right) → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Confirm. The CHEMATRIX icon appears on your home screen.
4. Open it once while still online and wait for the message "CHEMATRIX is saved for offline use".

## iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link in **Safari**. (Chrome and other browsers on iOS may not offer installation.)
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open CHEMATRIX from the home screen once while online.

Note: iOS can delete stored data of web apps that have not been opened for several weeks. If that happens, open the app once online and it downloads again (your history would be lost).

## Windows, Mac or Chromebook

1. Open the link in **Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar (a monitor with a down arrow), or the **Install the app** button on the welcome page.
3. CHEMATRIX opens in its own window and appears with your other apps.

Without installing, you can also just keep using it in a normal browser tab; offline use still works in that browser after the first visit.

## What works offline

Everything: all calculators, the equation balancer, reaction classifier and product predictor, step-by-step solutions, graphs and the particle simulation, the formula library, unit converter, scientific calculator, practice mode and history.

Internet is needed only:
- for the very first visit (to download the app, under 1 MB),
- to receive updates (the app shows "An update is ready" with a Reload button),
- after you clear the browser's site data for CHEMATRIX (it then needs one online visit again).

## Checking that it is ready

Settings → **Install and offline** shows whether the app is installed, whether it is saved for offline use, and whether you are online.

## Removing the app

- Android: long-press the icon → Uninstall / Remove.
- iPhone/iPad: long-press the icon → Remove App.
- Computer: open CHEMATRIX → menu (⋮) → Uninstall.

To delete your history and settings without uninstalling: Settings → **Clear all stored data**.
